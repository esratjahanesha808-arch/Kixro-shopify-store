> **Last Updated:** 2026-07-21

## Brand Name

**Kixro** — pronounced "Kicks-Row"

## Brand Positioning

A **youthful-premium** footwear brand that balances editorial sophistication with approachable energy. Not overly luxurious. Not cheap. Confident, clean, and modern.

- **Youthful** — energetic, contemporary, culturally aware
- **Premium** — quality craftsmanship, curated selections
- **Clean** — uncluttered, intentional, refined

---

## Color Usage Rules

1. **Background Cream (#FBF5EF)** is the default page background — never use pure white as the page background.
2. **Brand Blue (#1B4965)** is reserved for primary actions: buttons, active nav states, and key CTAs.
3. **Accent Teal (#4EC9C6)** is used sparingly for highlights, badges, and secondary interactive elements.
4. **Dark Text (#1A1A1A)** is the only text color on light backgrounds. Never use brand blue for body copy.
5. **White (#FFFFFF)** is used for text on dark backgrounds (hero overlays, dark sections, CTA banners).
6. Maintain a **minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio** for all text/background combinations.

## Tone of Voice

- **Confident** — not aggressive, not timid
- **Conversational** — approachable but not casual
- **Aspirational** — inviting but not elitist
- **Concise** — every word earns its place

## Photography Style

Based on the provided hero and product images:
- **Lifestyle shots:** Dynamic, youthful subjects in urban/outdoor settings
- **Product shots:** Clean backgrounds (cream/beige), product as hero, studio-quality lighting
- **Mood:** Energetic but refined, confident, aspirational
- **Color grading:** Warm neutrals with selective color pops
